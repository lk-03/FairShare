Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Welcome to FairShare ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use FairShare, we collect the following minimal information necessary to deliver our group expense splitting services:

  • Account & Identity Data: When you sign in with Google or Email, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL to create your profile and display your avatar to members in shared expense cohorts.
  • Financial & Ledger Data: Expense amounts, category designations, participant split allocations, settlement records, and UPI Virtual Payment Address (VPA) IDs entered optionally for direct P2P payments. FairShare does not store bank account passwords, debit/credit card numbers, or UPI PINs.
  • Collaborative Data: Group names, household shopping lists ("Needs / House Cart"), and discussion notes attached to group transactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Facilitate accurate mathematical bill splitting and greedy min-flow debt simplification.
  • Generate standard OS-level UPI intent deep links (e.g. upi://pay) for zero-fee peer-to-peer transfers.
  • Authenticate your sessions securely via Supabase and Google Sign-In.

3. Google User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

FairShare's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We strictly request non-sensitive identity scopes (email, profile, and openid).
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and stored securely using Supabase PostgreSQL infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS) policies enforcing group-level privacy isolation. Local caching utilizes encrypted device storage.

5. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may update your profile, change your UPI VPA, leave cohorts, or request complete account and data deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue or reach out via our GitHub repository: github.com/lk-03/FairShare.
